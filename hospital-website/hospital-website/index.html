About Us

Welcome to Lutfor Neture Diabetes and Drignisc Center

Lutfor Neture Diabetes and Drignisc Center brings together the characteristics of a world-class care facility — a wide range of specialists, modern equipment and technology, a calm ambience and dependable service quality.

Our center is built on the synergy of medical expertise and advances in digital health through paperless medical records. Skilled nurses, technologists and administrators work together to make every visit simple, safe and comfortable.

Our Mission

Excellent and cost-effective healthcare for our patients, continuing development for our staff, and partnership in promoting health for our nation.

Our Vision

To be a renowned organization at the leading edge of medicine, providing quality healthcare to meet our nation's aspirations.
