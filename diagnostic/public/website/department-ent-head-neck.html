Specialized Care at BSH

The ENT & Head Neck service provides comprehensive assessment and treatment for ear, nose, throat and related head-and-neck conditions. Our clinical team works with patients to understand their condition, provide appropriate investigations and guide them toward a suitable treatment plan.
